Privacy Policy — MelloMasterAutonomy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

MelloMasterAutonomy respects user privacy and operates strictly as a first-party publishing automation tool for the official @mellomaster1 TikTok channel.

1. Data Accessed

Through TikTok OAuth 2.0 scopes (user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.upload, video.publish), MelloMasterAutonomy accesses only the @mellomaster1 channel's public profile metrics and video publishing permissions.

2. Data Storage & Sharing

OAuth credentials are stored securely in encrypted private configuration and are never shared, sold, or disclosed to third parties. No third-party viewer personal data is collected or stored.

3. Revocation

Authorization can be revoked at any time directly through the TikTok mobile or web application settings under Security & Permissions.
